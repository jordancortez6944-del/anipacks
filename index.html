Build the atmosphere

Music & sound effects.

Find audio that fits the energy of your edit. Use the source library's own license terms for each track or effect.

Music recommendations

Match the sound to the scene.

  • Dark / intense edits: dark electronic, cinematic bass, tense ambient builds.
  • Fast fight edits: high-energy electronic, breakbeat, or instrumental rock.
  • Emotional edits: piano, atmospheric pads, soft strings.
  • Hype / transformation edits: orchestral percussion, rising synths, energetic instrumentals.
  • Adventure edits: uplifting cinematic, rhythmic percussion, bright instrumentals.

Try searching these moods and genres in the audio libraries below. Avoid assuming a popular song is free to reuse.

SFX recommendations

Layer these sound types.

  • Transitions: whooshes, swishes, reverse sweeps.
  • Impact moments: cinematic hits, low booms, short impacts.
  • Build-ups: risers, tension drones, heartbeat-style pulses.
  • Glitch edits: digital glitches, static bursts, UI beeps.
  • Atmosphere: wind, rain, room tone, distant ambience.

Keep effects short and balanced so they support the edit instead of overpowering it.
